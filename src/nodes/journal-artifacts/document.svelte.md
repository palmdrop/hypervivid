A small project based on *journal artifacts*: short phrases, words, or quotes that I jot down during the day. The artifacts are not extracts from my "actual" journals. Reading these tends to bring me back to that moment.

I started doing this as a simple meditative practice. A tiny ritual. I enjoy lists, and I was thinking about memory prompts. Sometimes you don't need much for a memory to re-surface. Scrolling through this list is easier and more fun than sifting through journal notes. 

The project is partly inspired by [still-here.net](https://still-here.net). I found this site a while back and I don't know what it means. I don't know who the author is or what the fragments represent. Perhaps the approach is similar to mine, or entirely different: is it a log book? an art project? I found the site oddly inspiring.
